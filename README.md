# The Long Game: Grain by Grain

A public, multi-page planning site for a 90-minute documentary about life, death, emergence, entropy and practical resilience.

The story begins with a grain of sand on Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island. It moves through household and community preparation, South East Queensland and Australian systems, global risks, artificial intelligence, deep time, mythology and science fiction. It returns to a small choice a person or group can make now.

Public site target:

https://auraofintelligence.github.io/grain-by-grain-documentary/

## Public pages

- `index.html` - the public doorway and documentary promise
- `runtime.html` - the seven-act, 90-minute story map
- `field-map.html` - a curated repo-to-scene research map
- `playbook.html` - the optional life-strategy game language
- `production.html` - a practical path from research to film
- `boundaries.html` - evidence, cultural care, privacy and safety boundaries

## Working documents

- `docs/90-minute-treatment.md` - the written treatment
- `docs/repo-source-map.md` - the short source-repo order and intended use
- `docs/evidence-register.md` - claim labels and first research questions

## Source posture

The Strange But True Field Library helped locate relationships across the local repo garden. It is a research index, not proof that a claim is current or correct. Film claims can be checked against primary and local sources during scripting.

The public site does not present this treatment as official Quandamooka, emergency-management, government, scientific or production advice.

## Local preview

The pages are plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. They can be opened directly, or served with a small local web server:

```powershell

```

Then open `http://localhost:8000/`.
