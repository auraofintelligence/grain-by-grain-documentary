Act 1

The pulse

Why does life keep making order while the universe also pulls things apart?

Day and night. Breath in and out. Birth and death. Building and decay. Yin and yang opens the film as a visual doorway, not a lecture. A grain of sand is worn down, carried, buried and reused.

PictureShore at dawn and dusk, tide lines, seed growth, rust, repair, hands and a town waking.
SoundA pulse made from tide, ferry engine, wind, insects and human breath.
Source shelfGrain by Grain, Civilisation of Sand, GAJRA Earth.
StatusPhilosophy and visual metaphor stay separate from scientific narration.
9:00-22:00

Act 2

The island is a world

What does an island reveal about the systems that keep ordinary life moving?

Follow a meal, medicine, a phone signal, electricity and a person across the bay. The ferry gateway makes dependence visible. Country is not a blank test site; living culture, ecology, authority and consent shape what can happen.

PictureGumpi ferry movements, deliveries, water, power, community places, wildlife crossings and dune edges.
Human centreA resident, worker or business owner describing one ordinary journey.
Source shelfGumpi Ferry Open Data Lab, Community Ledger, Grain by Grain, Wildlife Rescue.
StatusPlace facts and cultural framing are checked with suitable local and primary sources.
22:00-36:00

Act 3

When the weather arrives

Which preparations remain useful across storm, fire, flood, heat and blackout?

Storm-season jobs provide the grounded entry. A bushfire memory and severe-weather sequence show how quickly normal life can change. The story notices recurring needs without pretending the hazards are interchangeable.

PictureGutters, batteries, water, fire preparation, weather moving over the bay and a kiosk in normal use.
Story turnFear gives way to the six-capacity map: power, water, food, knowledge, shelter and familiar people.
Source shelfGrain by Grain threats and prepared pages, Disaster Kiosks, Shared Table, Minjerribah Resilience.
StatusSafety details are refreshed against current public guidance near filming and release.
36:00-50:00

Act 4

The storms without weather

What changes when the shock comes through trade, politics, technology or trust?

Empty shelves, supply disruption, war, pandemic isolation, cyber incidents and artificial intelligence can disturb ordinary life through different pathways. The film looks for shared consequences while keeping their causes and ethics distinct.

PictureFreight, shelves, a repair bench, dated headlines, network maps and people checking information together.
Human centreA repairer, shopkeeper, civic researcher or AI practitioner working with a present-day choice.
Source shelfTip Loop, Maker-Space, AI Trust Index, Community Wealth, P4A Civic Lab.
StatusCurrent affairs and technology claims carry dates and release-time source checks.
50:00-66:00

Act 5

The community skill tree

What can people practise in calm times that remains useful under pressure?

The film becomes participatory. Food sharing, repair, local energy, offline information, neighbour trust, creative media and shared records appear as optional quest lines. Preparation is gamified; harm is not.

PictureA meal, repaired object, rooftop, notice, learning circle, public screen and local event.
Story turnThe Community Ledger becomes a map of real-world side quests rather than a command centre.
Source shelfCommunity Ledger, Shared Table, Maker-Space, Clean Energy, Noticeboards and Trust Hub.
StatusProposals remain proposals and participation stays voluntary.
66:00-80:00

Act 6

Deep time and old stories

How can science, uncertainty, mythology and science fiction share a film without pretending to be the same evidence?

Solar storms, asteroid impacts and volcanic winters open the deep-time door. Old stories and modern contact narratives show how humans rehearse the unknown. Science fiction becomes a visible simulation space.

PictureNight sky, rock and mineral textures, public-domain archives, instrument data and marked simulations.
Story turnEstablished, modelled, disputed, speculative and story labels become part of the screen language.
Source shelfGrain by Grain threats, Cosmic Nexus, Strange Intelligence, Mineral Moonshots, space weather and Civilisation of Sand.
StatusExtraordinary claims do not borrow scientific authority; cultural stories stay with suitable authority and context.
80:00-90:00

Act 7

Play the next turn

If nobody controls the whole board, what is one useful move?

The scale folds back down: civilisation, nation, region, island, street, household, person, breath. Viewers meet possible next moves rather than a command. The close echoes the tide from the opening.

PictureA first quest written down, people choosing small projects, a screening, sunset becoming dawn.
Final lineEntropy continues. Life continues organising too. What might we build, protect, learn or imagine next?
